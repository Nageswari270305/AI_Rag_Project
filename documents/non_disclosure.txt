NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement is between ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd. and XYZ Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

1. Confidential Information

Confidential information includes business plans, technical information, customer information, and pricing information.

2. Use of Information

The receiving party may use confidential information only for evaluating the business relationship.

3. Confidentiality Period

The confidentiality obligations remain effective for 3 years after disclosure.

4. Return of Information

Upon request, the receiving party must return or destroy confidential information.

5. Exceptions

Information that is already publicly available is not considered confidential information.
